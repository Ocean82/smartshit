Preview before anything changes

Every AI suggestion shows an interactive diff preview first. Approve or reject with one click. Your data is never modified without explicit consent — building trust that no other AI spreadsheet tool offers.

Your data stays on your machine

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about pricing, privacy, and what the app can do.

Is smartsht free?

Yes. The Free plan includes the full spreadsheet, templates, Excel/CSV import and export, the formula auditor, and 3 AI questions per day. You can also bring your own API key (BYOK) for unlimited AI on your own provider account at no subscription cost.

How much is Pro?

Pro is $7 per month at the early adopter rate (listed vs $12/mo). It unlocks unlimited AI questions, unlimited auto-fix, unlimited cloud backup and sync, version history, and priority AI models.

Does my spreadsheet data stay private?

By default your spreadsheet data stays in your browser. Nothing syncs to smartsht servers unless you explicitly enable cloud backup. Self-hosted and BYOK setups are supported for users who want full control.

Can I import Excel files?

Yes. You can import existing .xlsx and CSV files and export back anytime. smartsht is designed to work alongside Excel and Google Sheets without vendor lock-in.

What does the formula auditor catch?

The auditor checks for issues like skipped cells in SUM ranges, inconsistent formula patterns, statistical outliers, and hardcoded constants — then can propose fixes with a preview before anything changes.
