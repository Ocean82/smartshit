## Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about pricing, privacy, and what the app can do.

### Is smartsht free?

Yes. The Free plan includes the full spreadsheet, templates, Excel/CSV import and export, the formula auditor, and 7 AI questions per day. You can also bring your own API key (BYOK) for unlimited AI on your own provider account at no subscription cost.

### How much is Pro?

Pro is $7 per month at the early adopter rate (listed vs $12/mo). It unlocks unlimited AI questions, unlimited auto-fix, unlimited cloud backup and sync, and version history.

### Does my spreadsheet data stay private?

The workbook stays in your browser unless you turn on cloud backup. The formula auditor runs locally. An AI question sends that question and a summary of relevant cells to our server, or to the provider on your own API key.

### Can I import Excel files?

Yes. You can import existing .xlsx and CSV files and export back anytime. Imported formulas keep Excel's saved values until you edit the cell.

### What does the formula auditor catch?

The auditor checks for issues like skipped cells in SUM ranges, inconsistent formula patterns, statistical outliers, and hardcoded constants — then can propose fixes with a preview before anything changes.

### Do I need to install anything?

No. smartsht runs in the browser. Open smartsht.com/app on Windows, macOS, Linux, or a mobile browser — no desktop install required.

### Is smartsht an Excel alternative?

Yes, for everyday work like budgets, invoices, and expense tracking. You get a real spreadsheet with formulas, charts, and pivot tables, plus an AI assistant. Import and export .xlsx so you can keep using Excel when you need to.

### Is this a Google Sheets alternative?

It is a browser spreadsheet you can use instead of Google Sheets for personal and small-business files. The workbook stays in your browser unless you turn on cloud backup. Collaboration is view-only share links today, not live multiplayer editing.

### What is an AI spreadsheet?

An AI spreadsheet lets you describe the sheet you want in plain English. smartsht builds or edits real cells and formulas, shows a preview by default before applying changes, and can audit formula errors. It is a full spreadsheet, not a chat window that dumps a table.
