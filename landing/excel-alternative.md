# Excel alternative for people who hate formulas

A free AI spreadsheet. Compared with Excel and Google Sheets.

smartsht is a browser spreadsheet you drive in plain English. Ask it to build a monthly budget, track expenses, or generate an invoice, and it writes the sheet. It is free to start, with no credit card. The workbook stays in the browser unless you turn on cloud backup.

It is not a clone of Excel's desktop app, and it does not include Copilot or Gemini. It is a lighter alternative when the job is a budget, an expense log, an invoice, or a formula check, and you do not want another install.

## Open Excel files without Excel

Import .xlsx, .xls and CSV files, edit them in the browser, and export back to .xlsx. Formulas, values and most cell formatting come across, and imported formulas keep Excel's saved values until you edit the cell. Macros (VBA) do not run. Because it is a normal browser tab, there is nothing to install and no license to buy to read or fix a spreadsheet someone sent you.

## A Google Sheets alternative that keeps files on your device

smartsht keeps the workbook in your browser by default, so a budget or expense log does not have to live in someone else's cloud. Turn on cloud backup when you want a copy on more than one device. Download a Google Sheet as .xlsx or CSV and open it here. View-only share links work today. Live multi-person editing does not.

## What you can do without learning formulas

- Describe a budget, expense tracker, or invoice and get a real sheet, not a chat transcript.
- Use normal spreadsheet formulas (SUM, VLOOKUP, IF, and the rest) when you want them.
- Run the [formula auditor](https://smartsht.com/formula-auditor) in the browser before you trust a file.
- Put AI functions in cells, such as `=AI.CLASSIFY` and `=AI.SUMMARIZE`.
- Preview edits before they apply. Auto mode applies low-risk edits and can be undone.
- Keep each workbook as its own file, sort files into folders, and restore anything you delete from Trash within 30 days.
- Export the audit as a single shareable report to send to a manager, client or teammate.

Pro ($7/month at the early adopter rate, or $59/year) adds unlimited AI questions, unlimited auto-fix, unlimited cloud backup, version history, and audit reports without the smartsht footer.

## When Excel or Sheets is still the better tool

Stay in Excel if you depend on desktop macros, a specific add-in, or a workbook that already runs a large VBA process. Stay in Google Sheets if several people need to edit the same file at once. smartsht share links are view-only.
