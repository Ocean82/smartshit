Excel alternative for people who hate formulas

A free AI spreadsheet. Compared with Excel and Google Sheets.

smartsht is a browser spreadsheet you drive in plain English. Ask it to build a monthly budget, track expenses, or generate an invoice, and it writes the sheet. It is free to start, with no credit card. The workbook stays in the browser unless you turn on cloud backup.

It is not a clone of Excel's desktop app, and it does not include Copilot or Gemini. It is a lighter alternative when the job is a budget, an expense log, an invoice, or a formula check, and you do not want another install.

What you can do without learning formulas

  • Describe a budget, expense tracker, or invoice and get a real sheet, not a chat transcript.
  • Use normal spreadsheet formulas (SUM, VLOOKUP, IF, and the rest) when you want them.
  • Run the formula auditor in the browser before you trust a file.
  • Put AI functions in cells, such as =AI.CLASSIFY and =AI.SUMMARIZE.
  • Preview edits before they apply. Auto mode applies low-risk edits and can be undone.

What stays free

The Free plan includes the full spreadsheet, templates, Excel and CSV import and export, the in-browser auditor, 7 AI questions per day, 3 auto-fixes, 1 cloud workbook, and view-only share links. Bring your own key if you want AI questions billed by your provider instead of the daily limit.

Pro ($7/month at the early adopter rate, or $59/year) adds unlimited AI questions, unlimited auto-fix, unlimited cloud backup, and version history.

When Excel or Sheets is still the better tool

Stay in Excel if you depend on desktop macros, a specific add-in, or a workbook that already runs a large VBA process. Stay in Google Sheets if several people need to edit the same file at once. smartsht share links are view-only.
